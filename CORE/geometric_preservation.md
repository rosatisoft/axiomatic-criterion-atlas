9\. Stability Classes



ACA distinguishes the following states:



State	Condition

Stable	low origin cost and positive orientation

Weak	low origin cost and weak orientation

Ambiguous	unclear field or near-zero orientation

Drifting	orientation decay across time

Inverted	low origin cost and negative orientation

Dispersed	high origin cost

10\. Geometric Preservation and Runtime Systems



Runtime systems may use preservation metrics to determine:



whether to allow reasoning,

whether to constrain reasoning,

whether to request clarification,

whether to monitor trajectory decay,

or whether to flag criterion drift.



Possible runtime policies:



Condition	Action

preserved	ALLOW

weak preservation	ALLOW\_LIGHT

ambiguity	CLARIFY

decay	MONITOR

inversion	FLAG\_DRIFT

dispersion	REJECT\_OR\_CLARIFY
